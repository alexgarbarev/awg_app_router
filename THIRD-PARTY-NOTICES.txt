the core binary `awgapprouter-box`.

1. amnezia-box — GPL-3.0
   https://github.com/amnezia-vpn/amnezia-box
   A fork of sing-box used as the tunnel core (TUN + AmneziaWG + Clash API).

   therefore distributed under the terms of GNU GPL version 3.
   Complete corresponding source: clone this repository and run
   `scripts/fetch-vendor.sh && scripts/build-core.sh`, or clone
   amnezia-box upstream at the commit recorded by fetch-vendor.

2. sing-box — GPL-3.0
   https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box
   Upstream of amnezia-box.

3. amneziawg-go — MIT
   https://github.com/amnezia-vpn/amneziawg-go
   AmneziaWG protocol implementation (Go), linked into the core.

4. wireguard-go — MIT
   https://git.zx2c4.com/wireguard-go
   Upstream lineage of amneziawg-go.

5. gVisor netstack — Apache-2.0
   https://gvisor.dev
